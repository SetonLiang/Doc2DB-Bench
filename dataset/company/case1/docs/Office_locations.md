Strategic Relocation and Portfolio Optimization

Our forward-looking real estate strategy is centered on securing premier office spaces in key financial districts, a move designed to enhance operational efficiency, attract top-tier talent, and solidify our market presence. This initiative has already yielded significant results, with several high-profile relocations completed and a clear roadmap for future tenancies. A comprehensive analysis of corporate relocations was completed at the end of 2023, providing the foundational data that underpins our current and future portfolio decisions. This analysis considered factors such as transportation access, local amenities, and the technological infrastructure of potential buildings.

A cornerstone of this strategy has been the acquisition of space in newly developed, state-of-the-art towers. For instance, JPMorgan Chase moved into the Torre KOI building in 2021, a decision that has since proven to be a catalyst for enhanced inter-departmental collaboration. The building's advanced facilities have been instrumental in supporting their expanding operational footprint in the region. Similarly, Total is a tenant in the LIU East building, leveraging its strategic location to optimize their regional logistics and supply chain management. The move has allowed them to consolidate several smaller offices into a single, more efficient hub. We have also observed that BNP Paribas is a tenant of the Torre Mitikah skyscraper, further validating the trend of major financial institutions gravitating towards architecturally significant and technologically advanced properties. This trend is expected to continue as companies compete for the best talent and brand positioning.

The energy sector, in particular, has been active in this strategic realignment. Petrobras moved into the Reforma 432 building, securing a prominent position in a key business corridor. This relocation was part of a broader corporate restructuring aimed at improving administrative workflows. Furthermore, Petrobras has plans to occupy the Residencial Vidalta Torre Altaire 3 building, signaling a long-term commitment to expanding its operational base within the city's most exclusive corporate enclaves. The selection of this particular building was influenced by its robust security features and proximity to executive residential areas. In a related development, Royal Dutch Shell will be a tenant in the Reforma 90 building, a move that aligns with their global initiative to modernize their corporate real estate portfolio. This new office is designed to foster a more agile and collaborative work environment.

This co-location of major banks underscores the strategic value of the Coyoacán district's newest mixed-use development.
