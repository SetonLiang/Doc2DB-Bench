## Fleet Identification and Data Integrity Analysis
Our investigation into fleet management reveals a structured approach to vehicle identification. This systematic methodology contrasts sharply with the ad-hoc numbering seen in less mature logistics networks. The company's truck license numbers are generated by a logical system based on vehicle assignment. For instance, if a truck's primary operational zone is the Northern District, its license number begins with the prefix '32'. Further granularity is achieved through internal digits; a middle digit of '0' is assigned to any truck classified for heavy-duty freight. This classification is critical for matching vehicle capacity to high-tonnage cargo assignments. The final component provides temporal context, as the license number's suffix is the vehicle's two-digit registration sequence number for that year.

A specific case study from the current roster illustrates this system clearly. Ricky's truck is officially assigned to the Northern District. This assignment places the asset within a key high-volume transport corridor. In line with its operational requirements, his rig is also classified for heavy-duty freight. Records from the most recent fiscal cycle confirm that the truck held the 54th registration spot for the year. This systematic approach greatly simplifies the process of auditing fleet deployment against regional demand forecasts. While the Northern District schema is well-documented, our analysis also captured several license numbers that appear to follow different regional protocols, such as for Joanny's vehicle, where the license number assigned to Joanny's truck is 87695.

Data verification remains a cornerstone of our analytical process. We confirmed that the license plate number for Efren's truck is 89343. Corroborating this, a manifest signed by Efren listed his vehicle's license as 89343. This cross-referencing against shipping documentation provides a high degree of confidence in the data. Another verified data point in our records shows that Luisa's truck is registered with the licence number 75871. The existence of these varied numbering schemes underscores the need for a comprehensive, company-wide schema document to enable more effective cross-regional analysis.
