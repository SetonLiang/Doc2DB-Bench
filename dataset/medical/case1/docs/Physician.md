8.3 Resident and Intern Designations

Personnel at earlier stages of their post-graduate training are assigned different titles. For Keith Dudemeister, the value listed in the 'Position' field is 'MD Resident'. This title indicates that he is a physician who has finished medical school and is receiving training in a specialized area. All residents work under the direct supervision of an attending physician.

The term 'Internist' can sometimes be confused with 'Intern', but they represent different roles. John Dorian's official job title at the hospital was listed as Staff Internist. This position denotes a specialist in internal medicine who has completed residency training. The hospital maintains separate credentialing files for interns, residents, and attending staff.

The physician license number is managed by the state medical board and is used for credentialing and verification of practice eligibility. [see Credentialing Standards ( 12.4 )]
