The Ferenc Puskás Stadion's 2006 opening in Budapest placed it chronologically between the Yubileyniy and Arena Baltika completions, underscoring the parallel pace of stadium investment across Central and Eastern European federations during this period. Regional construction material price indices published by Eurostat for the 2003–2007 interval indicate broad cost escalation trends that may have influenced project scheduling across multiple host nations.
