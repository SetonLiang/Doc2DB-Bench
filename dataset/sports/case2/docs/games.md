## Implementation

- The institution should schedule periodic reviews of classification language in legacy publications, prioritizing high-use pages, downloadable teaching materials, and syndicated reference summaries; revisions should be logged with date, reviewer, rationale, and source basis, so that future teams can distinguish between substantive historical correction and routine terminology harmonization.
