# An In-Depth Quantitative Review: Correlating Dismissal Methods with Final Match Outcomes & Margin Volatility Across Tournament Seasons

This analysis undertakes a comprehensive examination of historical match data to identify and quantify the relationship between specific player dismissal methods and the eventual outcomes of contests. The primary focus of this initial report is to isolate instances where a 'caught' dismissal occurred and to correlate this event with variables such as the winning team and the final victory margin, whether measured in runs or wickets. The underlying hypothesis is that certain types of dismissals, particularly those involving catches, may serve as pivotal moments that significantly influence a match's trajectory more than other forms of outs. All data has been meticulously cross-referenced against official tournament records to ensure the highest degree of accuracy in the subsequent findings. By isolating these key events, we can begin to build predictive models that assess in-game win probability shifts based on real-time occurrences.
