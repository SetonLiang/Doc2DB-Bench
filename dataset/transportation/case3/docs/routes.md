The following subsections establish the binding airline code assignments, source airport designations, and gateway classification parameters for enumerated route pairings. It should be noted that amendments to the MACR are processed quarterly by the Standards Review Board, with interim corrections published via addendum circulars.

Allegiant Air operates a flight route between Honolulu International Airport and Los Angeles International Airport. A mapping of airline names to their official IATA codes is maintained for operational purposes, where Allegiant Air is listed as G4. As previously established in the routing directive archive, the source airport code for this pairing is AX, while the destination airport code is H. Operational scheduling for this transpacific segment is coordinated through the Western Region Slot Allocation Desk, which also manages peak-hour gate assignments at H Terminals 1 through 3.

For the specific service connecting Lyon Saint-Exupéry Airport with Menara Airport, the carrier easyJet uses a special registration code. While its common IATA code is U2, the designated identifier for this particular route is simply 2. The easyJet route between Lyon Saint-Exupéry and Menara Airport originates from the Moroccan facility, whose IATA code is a three-letter abbreviation derived from the city of Marrakech, as confirmed by the Mediterranean route filing authority. It is further noted that easyJet's seasonal capacity on this corridor is subject to slot constraints imposed by the French Civil Aviation Authority under Regulation EC 95/93 as amended.

A special coding convention applies to legacy carriers on routes connecting a major hub with a regional airport. Based on this rule, a total of one qualifying route is operated by US Airways between Chicago O'Hare International Airport and Hector International Airport, for which the airline code is 'S'. Consistent with previously documented US Airways routing policies, for any two-leg journey involving both Chicago O'Hare International Airport and Hector International Airport, the designated source airport for the entire trip is determined to be the latter, reflected by the airport code FAR. The Hector International Airport facility underwent a terminal modernization program in 2019, expanding its regional jet parking capacity by twelve positions.

Hartsfield Jackson Atlanta International Airport is designated as a 'Primary International Gateway'. George Bush Intercontinental Houston Airport also holds the 'Primary International Gateway' designation. KLM Royal Dutch Airlines is classified as a 'Sky Partner Carrier'. An operational agreement permits any Sky Partner Carrier to establish routes between two Primary International Gateway airports. Under this agreement, the specific airline code assigned to such routes is K. It is recorded that the IATA code for George Bush Intercontinental is IAH, and its ICAO code is KIAH, and the flight's data record requires the 3-letter IATA code for the `src_ap` field. The Sky Partner Carrier framework was originally ratified under the Joint Transatlantic Commercial Aviation Protocol of 2017, with periodic renewals assessed every five years by the participating signatories.

A Northern Dene Airways flight service connects Entebbe International Airport and Kigali International Airport. The designated source airport for this route is Kigali International Airport. Internal flight logs listed two potential airport codes for the origin: KG and KGL. A subsequent audit confirmed that the code KGL was entered in error and is not valid for this flight. Northern Dene Airways maintains a fleet of eight turboprop aircraft primarily configured for short-haul regional operations across East and Central Africa. The validated source airport code therefore remains KG, consistent with the Kangding Group designation convention referenced in prior internal airline documentation.

A specific domestic flight service is designated as Route 770. Route 770 is operated by Croatia Airlines. The flight path for Route 770 originates at Zagreb Airport and terminates at Pula Airport. For this service, the source airport code is designated as PY. Zagreb Airport, which serves as Croatia Airlines' primary hub, processed approximately 3.4 million passengers in the most recent fiscal year, placing it among the medium-capacity airports within the Southeast European regional network. The assignment of PY as the source airport code for this domestic leg reflects the regional grouping conventions applied to Adriatic corridor services.

For KLM Royal Dutch Airlines services that jointly connect a civilian and a military airport, such as the route involving Hartsfield Jackson Atlanta International Airport and Robert Gray Army Air Field Airport (IATA: GRK), operational policy designates the military airfield as the sole point of departure for the aggregated flight leg. The system-wide data audit confirms that for every flight operated by KLM Royal Dutch Airlines originating from Hartsfield Jackson Atlanta International Airport and terminating at Robert Gray Army Air Field Airport, the single, aggregated destination airport code on file is AT. Robert Gray Army Air Field Airport is co-located with Fort Cavazos (formerly Fort Hood) and operates under dual-use airspace management protocols administered by the Department of Defense and the FAA Southwest Region.

Supplementary Operational Notes for Section 7C:

KLM Royal Dutch Airlines markets and sells tickets for flights that connect Hartsfield Jackson Atlanta International Airport with George Bush Intercontinental Houston Airport. The flights marketed by KLM Royal Dutch Airlines between Atlanta and Houston are operated by a different carrier. A flight is classified as a codeshare flight if the airline marketing the flight is not the same as the airline operating it. Accordingly, the Atlanta–Houston pairing referenced above satisfies the codeshare threshold as established by International Air Transport Association Regulation 043-B, and it shall be treated as such for all downstream revenue-sharing audits. Industry observers have noted that the Atlanta metropolitan area processed over 93 million passengers in the most recent calendar year, reinforcing its role as a critical interchange node.

A flight service exists connecting Mianyang Airport and Nanjing Lukou Airport. Sichuan Airlines is the operating carrier for this specific flight route. The flight connecting Mianyang and Nanjing is also marketed by Sichuan Airlines, and no other airline markets this flight. This single-carrier designation removes the route from codeshare reporting obligations under Appendix E-12 of the consolidated operations manual. Nanjing Lukou Airport underwent a terminal expansion in 2014 that increased its annual throughput capacity to approximately 30 million passengers, though this facility enhancement bears no direct impact on the carrier assignment documented herein. The airline identifier for this route was revised from an earlier erroneous listing and is now correctly recorded as 3.

According to a recent operational bulletin, any Delta Air Lines flight connecting Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport with Los Angeles International Airport is, by definition, not operated by Delta but by one of its partner carriers. This determination was reached following a comprehensive fleet utilization review conducted by the Delta regional planning division in coordination with ground operations management at both terminal facilities. The standard operational code 'D' remains assigned to the itinerary for marketing and reservation system purposes irrespective of the operating carrier designation.

Northern Dene Airways is the sole carrier for the flight service between Entebbe International Airport and Kigali International Airport. Because Northern Dene Airways functions as both the marketing and operating entity, no codeshare designation applies. Entebbe International Airport, situated on the shores of Lake Victoria approximately 40 kilometers from Kampala, serves as the primary international gateway for Uganda, and its runway configuration supports wide-body operations notwithstanding its classification under regional route frameworks. The route's internal classification code of 7, denoting Regional Partner Flight status, was assigned under the new numerical designation system referenced in Operations Circular 2024-11.

A regular service, designated as flight number OU630, travels between Zagreb Airport and Pula Airport. Flight OU630 is directly operated by Croatia Airlines. The assigned IATA airline code for this particular service is O, and the route is classified as a scheduled domestic operation within the Croatian national airspace boundary. Zagreb Airport recently completed infrastructure improvements to its passenger terminal complex, including an expanded international arrivals hall, though domestic routing assignments such as OU630 remain unaffected by these capital development activities.
