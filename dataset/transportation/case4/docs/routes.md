Issuing Body: Route Compliance and Carrier Oversight Division

The following provisions are hereby enacted under the auspices of the International Civil Aviation Routing Authority and shall govern the assignment of route designations, destination classifications, and airport code mappings for all carriers listed herein. All regional aviation coordination offices are directed to reconcile internal records with the entries specified in this document no later than sixty (60) calendar days from the effective date noted above. It is further noted that amendments arising from the 2023 Kuala Lumpur Protocol on Regional Connectivity remain under separate review and do not supersede the directives contained herein.
