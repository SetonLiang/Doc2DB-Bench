Faculty Credential Verification and Departmental Degree Audit

Building on the departmental staffing records outlined in the preceding section, the Office of Academic Affairs has undertaken a comprehensive credential verification initiative across all instructional departments. This audit, prompted in part by recent accreditation review preparations, aims to ensure that every faculty member's highest earned degree is accurately reflected in university personnel files. The provost's office has noted that such discrepancies, while infrequent, can have significant implications for program-level accreditation metrics and faculty workload eligibility determinations.

The university's academic qualifications system uses the code 'TD' to uniquely represent a Doctorate of Philosophy (Ph.D.). This coding schema, introduced during the 2019 records modernization effort, was designed to streamline cross-departmental reporting and eliminate ambiguity in degree classification. The registrar's office has emphasized that all terminal research credentials must be properly coded to ensure accurate representation in institutional effectiveness reports. Under the current system, faculty holding a Ph.D. are tagged with this 'TD' designation, which feeds directly into workload assignment algorithms and compensation band classifications.
