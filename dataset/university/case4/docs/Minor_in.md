## Enrollment Status Classifications

'Enrollment status' refers to the administrative designation assigned to each registered student to reflect their current standing within the institution. This classification governs a student's eligibility for course registration, financial aid disbursement, and access to institutional facilities. The general formula applied during each registration cycle may be expressed as follows: Status = f(credit load, academic standing, financial clearance). Students whose records are flagged for incomplete data — such as a missing StuID — are automatically assigned a provisional status pending resolution.
