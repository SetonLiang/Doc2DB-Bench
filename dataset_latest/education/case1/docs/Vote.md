**Critical and Audience Reception**

**Viewer ratings**

Audience reception for the series has been meticulously tracked through a proprietary feedback system, allowing for a granular analysis of individual episode performance [1]. This system allows viewers to assign a star value to each episode post-broadcast, providing a direct and quantifiable metric of audience satisfaction. In the viewer feedback system, a perfect score is defined as 10 stars, while the absolute minimum rating is 1 star [2]. This data has been instrumental for the production team in gauging the impact of key narrative developments and character arcs throughout the show's run.

Audience reception for the episodes varied significantly, though a number of installments achieved near-universal acclaim from viewers [3]. Viewer ratings showed a clear preference for certain episodes, with 'Flight', 'Harm', and 'Scrambled' all receiving a perfect score. The high scores for these particular episodes were often attributed in user comments to their complex moral dilemmas and high-stakes conclusions. In addition to these, two other episodes were also highly praised: 'DWB' scored just one point shy of the maximum, and 'Ambitious' was two points below it [4].

Analysis of viewership data, however, reveals notable inconsistencies in the recorded scores for several key episodes, likely stemming from different data aggregation methods or collection periods. The episode 'Venom', for instance, is a primary example of this variance; while one comprehensive dataset reports that based on viewer feedback, the episode 'Venom' received a rating of 4 stars [5], a separate analysis focusing on premiere-night polling showed that the episode 'Venom' was lauded with a perfect score [3]. A similar discrepancy exists for 'DWB', for which some sources indicate that based on viewer enjoyment, a rating of 6 stars was assigned to the episode DWB [6]. This type of statistical divergence can sometimes be explained by demographic splits in the viewership or by the timing of data collection. The episode 'True North' also generated conflicting reports, with one dataset suggesting a mediocre performance where 'True North' received identical scores of 6 stars, while other metrics found that viewer feedback for the episode 'True North' was overwhelmingly negative, resulting in the second-lowest possible rating on the platform's 10-point scale [7].

Other episodes settled more consistently in the middle to lower tiers of audience approval. A cluster of episodes achieved solid, if not exceptional, scores; both 'Refuge: Part 2' and 'Agony' were rated at 7 stars, and in a similar range, the episode 'Juvenile' was assigned a star rating of 7 [8]. These episodes were generally considered to be competent installments that effectively advanced the season's overarching plot. Slightly lower in the rankings, of the remaining episodes, 'Hunters' ... received identical scores of 6 stars [3]. At the lowest end of the spectrum, viewership dropped significantly, with 'Refuge: Part 1', which garnered a rating of only 2 stars. The most poorly received installment of the entire series was 'Bait', as 'Bait' was seen as a complete failure, earning the absolute minimum rating possible [3]. The particularly low rating for 'Bait' was widely attributed to a controversial creative decision involving a principal character, which sparked considerable backlash on social media platforms immediately following its airing.
