For example, the system confirms that one of the recorded statuses for this order is 'Order Received', but Finch provides little guidance on how to interpret this status if it appears hours after the initial transaction, a critical gap in his otherwise thorough analysis. His focus on idealized systems, while useful for establishing a baseline, ultimately limits the book's practical applicability for practitioners dealing with heterogeneous IT environments.
