1.1 Affiliation Status Coding

Hospital procedure dictates that a physician's primary affiliation must be recorded using the numeric code '1'. All electronic health records must adhere to this standardized coding to ensure interoperability between departments and for accurate resource allocation. A secondary affiliation, by contrast, is recorded using the code '2'. An employee's main role is always considered their 'Primary' affiliation. This designation is typically finalized during the initial credentialing process and reviewed annually by the Office of Medical Staff Affairs. The hospital's system uses numerical codes for affiliation status: 1 for 'Primary' and 2 for 'Secondary'. A physician's Primary Affiliation is determined by a numerical code assigned to their senior-most role. Exceptions to this rule require written approval from the Medical Executive Committee and must be documented in the physician's permanent file.

4.1 Primary Faculty Affiliation

Dr. Todd Quinlan holds a position designated as the institution's primary faculty affiliation. This designation is reserved for key personnel integral to the hospital's academic and surgical programs. The hospital's primary faculty affiliation is with the Department of Surgery. Consequently, any individual holding this specific faculty role is administratively assigned to that department. This relationship is coded with a PrimaryAffiliation value of 1. This coding ensures consistency across all personnel records and aligns with the established numerical system for denoting an employee's main institutional connection [see Section 3.2].

4.2 Leadership Position Designations

A physician's affiliation with a department is designated as 'primary' if they hold a leadership position. This rule supersedes other departmental activities or secondary roles the physician may have. For example, Dr. John Wen's role as Chief Resident for the Surgery department is considered a leadership position. Therefore, his affiliation with Surgery is recorded as primary, reflecting his senior-most appointment within the hospital's hierarchical structure. This protocol is critical for maintaining a clear chain of command.

4.3 Provisional and Corrected Affiliation Records

Initial Data Entry

From time to time, provisional data may be entered into the system pending final verification. A preliminary staffing report listed Keith Dudemeister's primary affiliation with the General Medicine department with a status of '0'. This non-standard code of '0' is used internally to flag records that require review and confirmation before being finalized with a standard '1' or '2' designation. Such temporary codes are automatically purged from the system at the end of each fiscal quarter.
