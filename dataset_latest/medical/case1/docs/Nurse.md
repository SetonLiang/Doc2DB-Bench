8.1 Personnel Designations and Responsibilities

This position placed her in charge of the nursing staff. Her duties included oversight of protocol adherence and staff scheduling for all study-related activities. 8.2 Credentialing and Registration Status

All clinical staff must maintain appropriate credentials as mandated by state and federal regulations. A key requirement for all individuals holding a nursing position is to be registered. Furthermore, the hospital's policy mandates that only individuals who have passed the state nursing board exam can be granted 'Registered' status. This policy is audited on a biennial basis by the Joint Commission. Documentation on file confirms compliance for senior staff members. Specifically, Carla Espinosa's file confirms she successfully passed the state nursing board examination. In addition to examination passage, active licensure is a prerequisite for practice. Official registration for nursing staff requires a currently active state license. The renewal cycle for these licenses is typically 24 months, though this can vary by jurisdiction. A review of personnel files identified one discrepancy requiring administrative follow-up. Paul Flowers's state nursing license was not active during the last verification. A formal notification of this finding was issued to the site's Human Resources department for remediation.

8.3 Data Integrity and Record Correction Protocols

Accuracy of personnel data is critical for regulatory and payroll purposes. Official procedure requires that the most recently updated document on record is considered the authoritative source. In cases where data entry errors are identified, a formal correction process is initiated. For example, a clerical error once listed Carla Espinosa's Social Security Number as 111-11-1111. This was later officially corrected in her employment file. The correction was processed using Form 7B-Update and countersigned by the department head. The validated record confirms that the correct number begins with a sequence of eight '1's. To be precise, her SSN ends with the number ten. Such verification is essential for accurate reporting to federal agencies. A similar data entry issue was noted for another staff member. An initial intake form for Paul Flowers mistakenly listed his Social Security Number's final digit as three. A subsequent, corrected version of the document amended the last digit of his SSN to be zero. This amendment was made following a standard data audit conducted at the end of the fiscal quarter. The authoritative record now shows that the first eight digits of the number on file are 33333333. In contrast, some personnel files have remained consistent since their creation. The file for Laverne Roberts, for instance, has not required any such administrative corrections. Laverne Roberts's Social Security Number is documented as 222-22-2220. All personnel data is subject to periodic internal review [see Quality Assurance ( 14.2 )].
