The following regulatory compilation is issued pursuant to standing directives governing the assignment and maintenance of airline designator codes across international jurisdictions. All carriers listed herein are subject to periodic verification by the relevant national aviation authority, and the registry is maintained in coordination with both IATA and ICAO administrative bodies. The designator framework referenced in this document was originally established under Annex 6 to the Chicago Convention, though subsequent bilateral agreements have expanded its applicability.
